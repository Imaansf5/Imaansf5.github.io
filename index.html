Introduction to Creative Computing – Year 0, UAL Creative Computing Institute, 2026/27

Week 1 – Getting started

  • Koch snowflake – my first p5.js sketch, duplicated and explored from a p5.js example.

Week 2 – Variables and data

  • Data Self Portrait – facts about me (age, height, sleep, languages) turned into shapes, inspired by Dear Data.

Week 3 – Drawing with code

  • Drawing my initials – the letters I and S made from rectangles and arcs.
  • Draw, then Code – a football pitch planned on paper first, then coded to match.

Week 4 – Conditions, movement and loops

  • Left or Right – the canvas changes depending on which half the mouse is on (if / else).
  • Billiard Ball – a ball that bounces off the edges of the canvas.
  • Seven Circles – seven evenly spaced circles drawn with a for loop.

Week 5 – Arrays and classes

  • Colour Observation – matching real colours (shirt, table, wall, hand) in RGB.
  • Living Line I – two 1D arrays (Joel's class version).
  • Living Line I – one 2D array (homework)
  • Living Line I – p5.Vector objects (homework)
  • Ripples in a Pond – a Ripple class and an array of objects.

Week 6 – Randomness and noise

  • Coin Toss – heads or tails picked at random from an array, with a running count.
  • Drunk Walk I: Brownian Motion – a walker taking random steps in any direction.
  • Drunk Walk II: Random Lattice Walk – random steps only up, down, left or right on a grid (homework).
  • Drunk Walk III: Smoothed Noise – smooth, flowing movement using Perlin noise (homework).
