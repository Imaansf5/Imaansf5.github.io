Imaan Saifi

BSc Creative Computing (Year 0) – Introduction to Creative Computing – Portfolio of p5.js sketches

Week 1

  • Koch Snowflake – a duplicated p5.js example of a fractal snowflake.

Week 2

  • Data Self Portrait – a portrait made from facts about me: 18 dots for my age, my height, sleep, languages, football, filmmaking and my favourite colour, pink.

Week 3

  • Drawing My Initials – my initials drawn with p5.js shapes.
  • Draw, then Code – a football pitch planned on paper first, then coded.

Week 4

  • Left or Right – the canvas reacts to which side the mouse is on.
  • Billiard Ball – a ball that bounces off the edges of the canvas.
  • Seven Circles – seven circles drawn with a loop.

Week 5

  • Colour Observation – four squares matching real colours around me: my shirt, the table, the wall and my hand.
  • Living Line I – Joel's class demo: a line that follows the mouse using two arrays.
  • Living Line I – 2D array – the same sketch rewritten with one 2D array.
  • Living Line I – p5.Vector – the same sketch rewritten with p5.Vector objects.
  • Ripples in a Pond – click to make ripples, using a Ripple class and an array of objects.

Week 6

  • Coin Toss – using random() to simulate heads or tails.
  • Drunk Walk I – Brownian motion: a point that takes small random steps.
  • Drunk Walk II – a random lattice walk that only moves in grid steps.
  • Drunk Walk III – a smooth walk using Perlin noise().

Week 7

  • Butt Generator – generates a random cartoon butt with arcs on every click (inspired by Le Wei's buttgenerator.com, from Code as a Creative Medium by Tega Brain and Golan Levin). My first version used bezier curves and hit a p5.js version 2 error with bezierVertex(), so I made a simpler arc version.
  • Circle using Sine and Cosine – a circle drawn point by point with x = cos(angle) and y = sin(angle), with no circle() function (inspired by Yuki Yoshida's drawCircle).
